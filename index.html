Privacy Policy

Operator and current service

CocoFlow is operated by Darbias Ibi, an independent developer acting as an individual, based in Tirana, Albania. Contact for privacy, deletion and support: djalljus2@gmail.com. Updated: 24 November 2026.

Scope and information processed

CocoFlow is currently a free private test platform in Albania for Instagram communication automation, order management and customer support. Authorized platform administrators and staff must be at least 18. Depending on the interaction, CocoFlow processes Instagram account identifiers and usernames; messages, timestamps and conversation history; images/attachments; names, phone numbers and delivery addresses; product photo, size, color, weight and preferences; and order, exchange, inquiry and support records. Administrators and staff also have account and permission information. Information comes from customers, authorized businesses and Meta APIs.

Purposes and responsibilities

Data is used to provide configured chatbot and staff conversations, manage orders/exchanges, answer inquiries, support customers and maintain service security. Businesses determine the purposes of processing their customer data; CocoFlow acts as processor when providing that service on their instructions. Darbias Ibi handles platform-account, support and security information needed to operate the test. Businesses must establish the lawful basis for their customer processing and provide appropriate notices. This page does not claim that Meta authorization alone establishes a legal basis for all customer data processing.

Hosting, recipients and security

Application data is currently stored in PostgreSQL on local hosting in Albania. Public legal pages are hosted by Cloudflare Pages and GitHub Pages; they contain no customer records, and Cloudflare and GitHub process website requests under their own privacy policies. Meta/Instagram process messaging data under their own terms and policies. Authorized business staff access data subject to business and staff permissions. Integration credentials and stored webhook payloads use application encryption; not every database field is encrypted. Private attachment handling and authentication are implemented. No claim of absolute security or certified compliance is made. Meta, Cloudflare and GitHub may process data internationally; this notice does not assert a particular transfer safeguard or hosting region for them. Contact the operator for questions before providing sensitive information.

Retention and deletion

The operator manually administers these retention periods: messages and images, 90 days; orders, 12 months; system logs and backups, 30 days. These are operational retention rules, not category-specific automatic deletion guarantees. The current application has an optional single per-business retention period for eligible closed records; active work and certain event/delivery states are protected. Manual review is needed to apply different periods, remove unlinked records and manage logs/backups. Deletion requests receive a response within 30 days. Specific records may be retained longer where necessary for a legal obligation or a documented security issue; the reason and scope should be explained to the requester. Attachment deletion can require retries. Backup copies are handled separately under the 30-day backup period; a restore requires reapplying relevant deletion actions.

Requests, rights and updates

Email djalljus2@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion, raise a privacy concern, or ask about other rights available under applicable law. Identify the business and account involved without sending passwords or tokens. The operator may ask for proportionate identity verification and coordinate with the relevant business. You may contact the competent data-protection authority in Albania under applicable law. See Data Deletion for the practical process and its limits. Material policy changes will be dated on this page and communicated by email to affected registered test participants where contact details are available.
