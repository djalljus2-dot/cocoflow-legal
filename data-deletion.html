Data Deletion Instructions

Contact and request

CocoFlow is operated by Darbias Ibi, an independent developer acting as an individual, based in Tirana, Albania. Contact for privacy, deletion and support: djalljus2@gmail.com. Updated: 24 November 2026.

How to request deletion

Email djalljus2@gmail.com with the subject CocoFlow data deletion. State the business you contacted, your Instagram username and approximate conversation or order dates. Do not send passwords, access tokens or App Secrets. The operator will respond within 30 days and may request only the information necessary to verify identity and locate records. For business-managed customer data, the operator coordinates with that business. This page provides instructions; opening it does not submit or execute a deletion request.

What deletion covers

Authorized administrators review the customer erasure preview and confirm deletion of linked customer, conversation, message and event data. Personal fields in linked operational records are removed and private files are queued for deletion. Unlinked records need separate review; failed file deletions need retries. Operational IDs/statuses and keyed pseudonymous replay-suppression values may remain. The operator will explain completion, outstanding work and any legal/security retention exception by email. No claim is made that all information is instantly or irreversibly erased from every system.

Retention and backups

The operator manually administers these retention periods: messages and images, 90 days; orders, 12 months; system logs and backups, 30 days. These are operational retention rules, not category-specific automatic deletion guarantees. The current application has an optional single per-business retention period for eligible closed records; active work and certain event/delivery states are protected. Manual review is needed to apply different periods, remove unlinked records and manage logs/backups. Deletion requests receive a response within 30 days. Specific records may be retained longer where necessary for a legal obligation or a documented security issue; the reason and scope should be explained to the requester. Attachment deletion can require retries. Backup copies are handled separately under the 30-day backup period; a restore requires reapplying relevant deletion actions.

Disconnect Instagram and revoke access

A business administrator can use the business dashboard > Integrations > local disconnect action. CocoFlow removes the local credential, cancels pending events and queued replies and disables local automation. To revoke access at Meta, separately remove CocoFlow from the relevant Instagram connected-app or Meta Business Integrations settings. Local disconnect does not itself revoke Meta permissions or delete historical customer records. Ask for data deletion separately. CocoFlow cannot erase messages or copies held independently by Meta, recipients or other businesses; contact those parties for their own deletion procedures.

Cfare perfshin fshirja

Administratoret e autorizuar shqyrtojne parapamjen dhe konfirmojne fshirjen e te dhenave te lidhura te klientit, bisedes, mesazheve dhe eventeve. Hiqen fushat personale nga regjistrat operativë te lidhur dhe skedaret privatë vendosen ne radhe fshirjeje. Regjistrat e palidhur kerkojne kontroll vecmas; fshirjet e deshtuara kerkojne riprovim. Mund te mbeten ID/statuse operative dhe vlera pseudonime me celes kunder riluajtjes se eventeve. Operatori shpjegon me email perfundimin, punen ne pritje dhe perjashtimet ligjore/sigurise. Nuk premtohet fshirje e menjehershme dhe e pakthyeshme nga cdo sistem.

Ruajtja dhe backup-et

Operatori i administron manualisht keto afate: mesazhet dhe imazhet, 90 dite; porosite, 12 muaj; logs te sistemit dhe backup-et, 30 dite. Keto jane rregulla operative ruajtjeje, jo garanci fshirjeje automatike sipas kategorise. Aplikacioni aktual ka nje afat opsional te vetem per biznes per regjistra te mbyllur te pershtatshem; puna aktive dhe disa gjendje eventesh/dorezimi mbrohen. Kerkohet kontroll manual per afate te ndryshme, regjistra te palidhur, logs dhe backup-e. Kerkesat per fshirje marrin pergjigje brenda 30 diteve. Regjistra te vecante mund te ruhen me gjate kur nevojiten per detyrim ligjor ose ceshtje sigurie te dokumentuar; arsyeja dhe shtrirja duhet t’i shpjegohen kerkuesit. Fshirja e bashkengjitjeve mund te kerkoje riprovim. Kopjet backup trajtohen vecmas sipas afatit 30-ditor; pas rikthimit duhen rizbatuar fshirjet perkatese.

Shkeputja e Instagram dhe revokimi

Administratori mund te perdore panelin e biznesit > Integrimet > shkeputjen lokale. CocoFlow heq kredencialin lokal, anulon eventet ne pritje dhe pergjigjet ne radhe dhe caktivizon automatizimin lokal. Per revokim ne Meta, hiqni vecmas CocoFlow nga aplikacionet e lidhura ne Instagram ose Meta Business Integrations. Shkeputja lokale nuk revokon lejet Meta dhe nuk fshin historikun e klienteve. Kerkoni fshirjen vecmas. CocoFlow nuk mund te fshije mesazhe ose kopje te mbajtura ne menyre te pavarur nga Meta, marresit ose biznese te tjera; kontaktoni ato pale per procedurat e tyre.
